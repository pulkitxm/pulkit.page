01 / The context

The real problem
comes first.

Edith is my native macOS control center. It brings together machines, clipboard history, music, calendar, and other everyday utilities. When one product has that much surface area, the interface has to make the next action feel easy to find.

This study explores an overview-first visual direction for Edith. A persistent sidebar provides orientation, while compact modules keep different kinds of information distinct. These are illustrative interface compositions with synthetic data, not screenshots of the shipping application.
