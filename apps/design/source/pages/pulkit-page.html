01 / The context

The real problem
comes first.

My main portfolio is where my projects, writing, experience, and conversations come together. It is a place to share what I am making and what I am learning, with enough structure to help someone find their way around.

This study explores a warmer editorial presentation of that existing content. Familiar navigation, a restrained reading width, and a visible human presence do the work. The compositions here are a design exploration; the current live website has its own visual style.
