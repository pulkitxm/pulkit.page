## Scope and timelines

Fixed price against a scope we agree up front. Ongoing work can run as a monthly retainer instead.

| Scope             | What it covers                                        | Typical timeline |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Landing page      | One page, messaging and structure, analytics wired up | 1 to 2 weeks     |
| Full website      | Multi-page site, component library, optional CMS      | 2 to 4 weeks     |
| Web or mobile app | Auth, dashboards, backend APIs, cross-platform mobile | 4 to 8 weeks     |

Every project ships with readable code, responsive design, search metadata and Core Web Vitals handled, a production deployment, documentation and a walkthrough, thirty days of post-launch support, and full ownership of the source.

## Questions

<details>
<summary>How long does a project take?</summary>

A landing page is usually one to two weeks, a full website two to four, a larger app four to eight. You get a specific date once I understand the requirements, not before.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can you work on something that already exists?</summary>

Yes, and plenty of the work is exactly that: redesigns, performance, bug fixes, new features, search. We audit what you have and agree on what's worth changing. My [past work](/exp/) is mostly this.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How does pricing work?</summary>

Fixed price against an agreed scope for most projects. After the first call you get a written proposal with the scope, the timeline, and the cost. Longer engagements can run as a monthly retainer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you help with copy and content?</summary>

I'm not a copywriter, but I'll push back on messaging and structure that won't land, and help you work out what belongs where.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens after launch?</summary>

Thirty days of support for fixes and small adjustments comes with every project. After that, maintenance and new features can carry on month to month.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the first step?</summary>

A [thirty minute call](https://cal.com/pulkitxm), free. We go through the project, the timeline, and the budget, and I tell you honestly whether I'm the right person for it.

</details>
