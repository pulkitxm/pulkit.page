## How to explore the archive

The standalone writing mixes practical implementation tutorials (React UI, Next.js, Node, Python, deployment, storage), developer workflows (Git and AI tools), and personal project stories. Kaksha follows the design of an offline school timetable app for the author's mother, with screenshots of grids, clashes, teachers, and sharing. My First Talk documents Claude Directory's launch and the first stage demo, including audience reactions and event photographs.

System Design combines explanations, diagrams, comparisons, implementations, and hands-on exercises: transactions and scaling, cache design, queues/streams, balancing, resilience, networking, and probabilistic structures. Its index explicitly links supplementary examples in the external `pulkitxm/systems` repository. Design Engineering progresses across motion judgment, physics/easing, rendering performance, transforms, transitions, keyframes, and clipping. Those articles retain static explanations and code, while original interactive demonstrations are outbound links.

The article topics do not imply that this portfolio runs those technologies. A post about Prisma does not introduce a database dependency; an article about Resend does not make the current Contact page an email API; the Next.js SEO posts do not implement metadata features in the static generator.
